# Download candidates: 100 papers (1976–2026)

"OA?" is a guess at whether a legal free copy exists: **arXiv**, **NTRS** (NASA Technical Reports Server), **OA** (open-access publisher or author/institutional repository), **paywall** (probably only through Monash library). The guesses get checked during download.
