## johnston-2016-underexpanded-jet-intrusive-tab-visualisation (queued 2026-10-23)

Johnston & Edgington-Mitchell 2016 is the library's only round-jet staging map with an azimuthally local lip change: one radial triangular tab (0.128D deep, just over 2% blockage) on a 15 mm converging nozzle, NPR 2.1–4.5 in 0.1 steps (Fig. 2, p. 53). The baseline jumps at NPR 2.4, 3.0 and 3.8 with the A → B down, B → C up, C → D down pattern of [Powell 1992](../notes/powell-1992-oscillation-modes-choked-circular-jets.md) (p. 2825); the tab raises St at every NPR, delays the first jump by 0.1 NPR, makes the jumps smaller, and removes both the upward B → C jump and the C → D drop (p. 53–55; note's digitisation). At NPR 2.6 two orthogonal views of two-point schlieren correlations show the baseline's antisymmetric B flapping replaced by a pattern symmetric in both planes, weaker on the tabbed side, the authors' "punctuated toroid" (p. 56–58): a loop change at fixed NPR set by the lip. It supports current-understanding point 4 (the staging NPR belongs to the rig) but is not the clean receptivity test the "Lip, exterior and reflectors" section asks for: unlike [Weightman 2019](../notes/weightman-2019-nozzle-external-geometry-azimuthal-mode-impinging-jet.md) (tones fixed) and André's notches ([André 2013](../notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), cells unchanged, p. 666–668), the tab truncates the shocks on its side and shortens the cells by about 16% (≈ 1.07D → 0.90D, note's reading of Figs 4–5), and every tone moves. By the note's arithmetic the tabbed first and third branches sit 1.18–1.20 times above baseline A and C, about what the shorter cells alone would give (Powell's f ∝ 1/L_s), while the second branch is about 1.5 times baseline B, exactly where the B → A-like change was imaged; this hints that the tab mostly rescales loops and replaces only B, but the spacing was measured only at NPR 2.6. The baseline A branch also falls 0.64 → 0.51 over NPR 2.1–2.3 with no upward A1 → A2 jump, below the same lab's A2 at St 0.63–0.65 at NPR 2.25 ([Edgington-Mitchell 2018](../notes/edgington-mitchell-2018-upstream-acoustic-modes-screech-closure.md)), more evidence that tip details move low-NPR staging. The open question "No free-jet test has changed only the exterior and tracked the A1/A2 limits" still stands; snapshot correlations cannot show whether the tabbed jet alternates between loops near its jumps (cf. [Knast 2018](../notes/knast-2018-coupling-modes-underexpanded-twin-axisymmetric-jet.md), p. 6).
