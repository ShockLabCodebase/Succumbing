## lu-2026-rectangular-jet-screech-dimensional-reduction (queued 2026-10-22)

- **Staging limits.** The paper's closing message bears directly on staging-limit prediction (current understanding item 5, "No model predicts the staging limits"). Reduced (planar) models predict screech frequencies but cannot give "the cut-on and cut-off frequencies of the different stages", because their GJM bands are too wide (p. 21). The 2D model with a measured base flow gives narrow bands and fewer spurious tones but is very sensitive to the base flow (p. 20–21).
- **Second band.** In an AR 2 rectangular jet, a second, higher band from NPR ≈ 3.5 is captured with the suboptimal shock wavenumber k_s2 (p. 20). This adds support for k_s2 loops in non-round jets alongside [Nogueira 2025](../notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md). The paper calls it "mode staging", but in [Mazharmanesh 2025](../notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md)'s data the loops coexist (multimodal) rather than replace each other, and the predicted k_s2 frequency at NPR 4.0 sits between the bi-axial and varicose tones (note's comparison).
- **Effect on the page.** The paper does not change current understanding, but it backs the "Multimodal loops and non-round jets" paragraph and adds a modelling reason why staging limits need realistic band edges.
- **PyFR LES.** This fits the PyFR plan to compute finite-thickness GJM bands on each converged LES mean at the SPOD-peak station (note's suggestion).
