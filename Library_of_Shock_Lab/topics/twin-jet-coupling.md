# Twin-jet coupling

_Last updated: 2026-10-21 · 13 papers_

## Scope
Pairs of supersonic, screeching jets, round or rectangular: the coupling states they lock into (symmetric or antisymmetric, in phase or out of phase, the D2 classes SS, SA, AS and AA), intermittency and switching between those states, the symmetry decompositions used to separate them, and how well twin-jet linear models (coupled vortex sheets, Floquet-in-azimuth LSA, KH preference maps) and LES match experiment.

What each jet does on its own (flapping against wagging, axis switching, single-jet models) is on [non-axisymmetric-jets](non-axisymmetric-jets.md), which also explains why a second jet, like an elliptical nozzle, turns helical motion into flapping. Staging and mode switching of round jets are on [screech-staging-and-mode-switching](screech-staging-and-mode-switching.md) and the screech loop on [screech](screech.md). Model formulations are on [linear-models](linear-models.md), SPOD/POD practice on [modal-decomposition](modal-decomposition.md), Hilbert and wavelet tracking on [time-frequency-methods](time-frequency-methods.md), and clustered rocket nozzles on [rocket-noise-models](rocket-noise-models.md).

## Current understanding
1. **Twin jets are coupled out to large spacings, but the coupling is often unsteady.** Steady coupling, switching between symmetries, one jet oscillating alone, and uncoupling all occur in round twins, at both the A1/A2 and the m = 1 stages ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md), p. 9–12; [Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 23–29; [Knast 2018](../notes/knast-2018-coupling-modes-underexpanded-twin-axisymmetric-jet.md), p. 4–5). In rectangular twins, AA coupling is broken by bursts of AA/AS competition ([Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md), p. 9–10).
2. **Symmetric coupling sits at a lower frequency than antisymmetric**, as twin-jet stability models predict, but the split is 0.3–0.6 % at A1/A2 and 5–15 % at m = 1 ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md), p. 19; [Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 27; [Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md), p. 20, 22). The NPR of each state differs between rigs ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 6, 17–18, 35).
3. **Each jet flaps as a single jet would, and coupling barely moves the tone.** Each jet of a rectangular pair flaps across its minor axis ([Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 9; [Yeung 2022](../notes/yeung-2022-3d-spod-twin-rectangular-jet.md), p. 8), and twin tones match those of single jets from the same nozzles within about 0.02 in St (below). Why a second jet produces flapping at all is set out on [non-axisymmetric-jets](non-axisymmetric-jets.md) ([Rodríguez 2023](../notes/rodriguez-2023-preferred-flapping-round-twin-jets.md)).
4. **The upstream guided jet mode closes the loop.** Its existence band explains symmetry selection better than KH growth does ([Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md), p. 15–19; [Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md), p. 18–19; [Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md), p. 20–21), but the bands admit both SS and SA, and no model yet picks the coupling state.
5. **Split the data by symmetry before decomposing it.** The split is exact and orthogonal, and it saves memory and speeds convergence ([Yeung 2022](../notes/yeung-2022-3d-spod-twin-rectangular-jet.md), p. 5, 8; [Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md), p. 6–8).
6. **Three diagnostic traps recur.** Two slightly detuned jets beat, which looks like intermittency or "coupling" in both symmetries ([Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 10–11; [Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md), p. 32–38). When two tones coexist, a cross-spectral phase read at one peak is spurious despite high coherence ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 9–10). Line-of-sight integration cancels out-of-phase content ([Esfahani 2021, control](../notes/esfahani-2021-control-twin-rectangular-jet-coupling.md), p. 15).

## Symmetry decompositions
- **D2 split (3-D data).** Four orthogonal components (SS, SA, AS, AA) sum exactly to the field, and SPOD of one quadrant per component saves 4× memory; full-field SPOD does recover the components eventually, but its fourth mode was unconverged ([Yeung 2022](../notes/yeung-2022-3d-spod-twin-rectangular-jet.md), p. 5, 8). Applied to near-lip pressure, the split gives instantaneous AA and AS amplitudes ([Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md), p. 10). Its use on single jets, with sub-domains, is on [non-axisymmetric-jets](non-axisymmetric-jets.md).
- **Symmetry-imposed POD (planar PIV).** Splitting into even and odd fields about the midplane works without time resolution, but the measurement plane cannot see two of the four symmetries (1AA, 1AS) ([Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md), p. 6–8, Table 1 p. 7).
- **Sub-domain and conditional POD (planar PIV).** POD of each jet's half of the field, then POD of only the snapshots in which one or both jets oscillate strongly, separates single-jet, symmetric and antisymmetric states without time resolution ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 22–27). Requiring both jets to be strong confines the symmetric and antisymmetric amplitudes to an arc, so the empty corners of the mutual-exclusivity plot are partly built in; the different wavelengths of the two coupled pairs are the firmer evidence (the note's analysis).
- **Half-domain and symmetry-imposed POD with CWT (TR schlieren).** One field per jet (HD) and even and odd fields (SI) are each passed through POD and a Morlet transform, which tracks each jet's frequency and the dominant symmetry over time ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md), p. 8–10).
- **Path-integrated views.** Schlieren through two out-of-phase jets cancels their lobes ([Esfahani 2021, control](../notes/esfahani-2021-control-twin-rectangular-jet-coupling.md), p. 15). Two-view fingerprints of single-jet modes are on [non-axisymmetric-jets](non-axisymmetric-jets.md).

## Coupling states and intermittency
**Round twins, m = 0 (A1/A2).** Staging NPRs barely differ from those of a single jet (staging itself is on [screech-staging-and-mode-switching](screech-staging-and-mode-switching.md)). Three states occur ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md), p. 1, 9–19): steady coupling in one symmetry; SS↔SA competition, with switches every O(10²–10³) cycles and a frequency shift of 0.30–0.63 %; and uncoupling, seen only at s/D 6, with the jets about 0.5–0.6 % apart in frequency. Every spacing changes state at the A1→A2 jump (p. 12). SS sits at a lower frequency than SA (p. 19), as twin-jet stability models predict ([Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md), p. 20, 22); this is the clearest model–experiment agreement on coupling. Fine NPR sweeps show that supply-pressure drift explains the switching at one condition but not at another, which points to stochastic switching between competing global modes ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md), p. 39–41).

**Round twins, m = 1 (higher NPR).** At S/D 3, [Knast 2018](../notes/knast-2018-coupling-modes-underexpanded-twin-axisymmetric-jet.md) find antisymmetric flapping, then counter-rotating helical motion, then symmetric flapping as NPR rises from 2.3 to 5.5; at S/D 6, co-rotating helical motion and symmetric flapping (Table 2, p. 9). The inter-plume phase jumps without any jump in frequency, coupling still dominates at S/D 6 (p. 4–5), and U_c/U_j stays at 0.57–0.60 across all the modes (p. 10). The "helical" labels rest on a two-point correlation that cannot rigorously tell a helix from flapping (p. 6–9). Knast attributed regions of scattered phase to unsteady coupling but could not test it (p. 5).

[Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md) test it, on the same records and on PIV from a second Monash rig:
- In the s/D 3 "phase anomaly" (NPR 3.4–4.4), coherence at all frequencies reveals two or three coexisting tones of continuously varying frequency. In their wide states the Hilbert envelopes dip more than 5 dB below the mean about 20 % of the time (p. 8–14). The dips are sometimes anti-correlated between the two jets and sometimes shared (p. 14–16), and the tones of different processes are uncorrelated rather than mutually exclusive (p. 16), unlike the two flapping tones of an AR 2 ellipse ([Mazharmanesh 2026](../notes/mazharmanesh-2026-triadic-interaction-mean-flow-deformation-screech.md), p. 14–15).
- Bell's conditional POD of about 9500 PIV fields at NPR 4.6 finds three states: one jet oscillating alone, symmetric coupling, and antisymmetric coupling, never both coupled forms at once (p. 23–28). The schlieren clips show either jet alone, both, or neither (p. 28–29).
- The symmetric tone again lies below the antisymmetric one: St 0.22 against 0.23 at NPR 4.6, matching POD wavelengths of 3.1D and 2.8D (Table 2, p. 27), and about 0.04 lower on the first rig (figure reading).
- The antisymmetric → indeterminate → symmetric sequence holds on both Monash rigs, which have identical nozzles, and at OSU, but at different NPRs, traced to enclosure reflections and the plenum face (p. 6, 17–18, 35–36).

So Knast's counter-rotating "helical" region is a mixture of states: the m = 1 counterpart of Wong's three A1/A2 states, with uncoupling already at s/D 3. The 5–15 % frequency split is an order of magnitude larger than Wong's; with the different wavelengths, it suggests a different loop closure per symmetry rather than a change in phase speed (the note's inference). At NPR 5 the coupling is steady and symmetric, the 1SS coupling that [Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md) find in the same PIV data (p. 9–12, 20–21).

**Rectangular twins.** Raman and Taghavi's AR 5 jets flap out of phase and switch to in phase at M_j ≈ 1.5, taking a new shared frequency; the out-of-phase mode is about 15 dB quieter midway between the nozzles ([Raman 1998](../notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 88–91; AR from [Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 3). In the OSU AR 2 C-D jets, each jet flaps at every M_j from 1.20 to 1.85, with no symmetric or lateral mode (p. 9). The pair ([Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 8–11):
- couples out of phase at M_j 1.40–1.50, loses coherence just above design, and couples in phase from M_j 1.65;
- gains only about 5 dB at the switch, against 15–20 dB in earlier rigs;
- at M_j 1.20–1.35 keeps two frequencies (140 Hz apart, falling to 33 Hz) and beats, which contradicts the shared-frequency claim.

In LES of the Cincinnati geometry (NPR 3, M_j ≈ 1.36), plateaus of AA coupling are broken by bursts in which AA and AS compete ([Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md), p. 9–10). Their tone, St 0.37 (p. 5), equals the 0.36–0.37 of the cold single jet from the same nozzle family ([Gojon 2019](../notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), Table 4, p. 24), and a single jet of the OSU design tracks the OSU twins within about 0.02 in St ([Nogueira 2025](../notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), the note's comparison with [Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md) Fig. 6, p. 8). Coupling therefore barely moves the tone. In the OSU geometry at M_j 1.5, LES puts the St 0.3 tone only in AA and AS, the components antisymmetric about the plane of the nozzles, with AA the stronger by figure reading ([Yeung 2022](../notes/yeung-2022-3d-spod-twin-rectangular-jet.md), p. 8). The minor-axis tone adds about 5 dB to the minor-axis OASPL ([Brès 2021](../notes/bres-2021-les-twin-rectangular-jets-plasma.md), p. 11).

Plasma forcing at the screech frequency moves the coupling ([Esfahani 2021, control](../notes/esfahani-2021-control-twin-rectangular-jet-coupling.md), p. 13–16). Symmetric, in-phase forcing removes the intermittency and raises the tone by 8 dB. Antisymmetric, out-of-phase forcing decouples the jets and lowers the tone by 7 dB, but raises the first harmonic by 14 dB. Decoupling coincides with screech being disrupted on one jet, so the effect of loop timing is not isolated. The same rig's regime boundaries move with stagnation and ambient temperature (p. 9).

## Linear models against experiment and LES
**Floquet LSA on a measured mean flow** ([Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md)). The KH wavenumber is 1.80 from POD against 1.90 from LSA; the upstream wavenumber is −1.93 in both, and KH shapes come out too narrow (p. 19–20). The four KH symmetries share one phase speed and differ only slightly in growth (p. 15–17). Only 1SS and 1AS have usable guided-mode bands near the tone (p. 18).

**Twin-jet vortex sheets and spacing.** [Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md) couple two vortex sheets through the Bessel addition theorem and also solve a finite-thickness model. Branch points of the SS (0, 2) mode bound A1 and A2 sharply from below (p. 18–19), and the best-fit finite-thickness parameters are identical to the single jet's (p. 19). The SA band narrows at small spacing, and the SA branch point comes within 1 % of the single-jet value only at S ≈ 50 (p. 11–12). The model still cannot pick SS or SA (p. 22). [Stavropoulos 2024](../notes/stavropoulos-2024-upstream-waves-merging-twin-jets.md) explain the spacing dependence geometrically: as twin jets merge, the (0, 2) mode becomes a higher-order mode of a double-width jet (p. 7), and touching round twins behave like an AR 2 ellipse at long wavelengths (p. 11–12). The SA branch point rises as the jets approach, while SS barely moves (p. 9).

**KH-only maps.** [Rodríguez 2023](../notes/rodriguez-2023-preferred-flapping-round-twin-jets.md) map the preferred mode for perfectly expanded jets: it falls in stripes of constant St·s/D, mostly SA1, and coupling strengthens with M_j and T_R (p. 12–19). The authors defer to guided-mode closure for screeching jets (p. 19–20), and the note reads their thin-shear-layer map as favouring SA1 where Nogueira observed 1SS.

**LES.** A blind wall-modelled LES of the OSU twins matches near-field spectra to St ≈ 1 and OASPL to within about 1 dB, but is 2–4 dB low on the minor axis at sideline and upstream angles ([Brès 2021](../notes/bres-2021-les-twin-rectangular-jets-plasma.md), p. 9–13). [Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md) run no LSA, but find that at the tone the guided-mode gain (10.10) far exceeds the free-stream acoustic gain (1.16), with a phase speed of −0.71 U_j (p. 18–20); their cross-excitation return points line up with guided-mode points (p. 21).

**Verdict.** Local models get wavenumbers right, the guided mode better than KH, and existence bands bound the tones, including the A1/A2 limits. No model yet predicts which coupling state wins, or the amplitude. All the models are locally parallel, and the twin-jet versions superpose single-jet mean flows, which holds for about 5 D in close jets ([Rodríguez 2023](../notes/rodriguez-2023-preferred-flapping-round-twin-jets.md), p. 8).

## Disagreements and open questions
- **What selects the coupling symmetry?** Three candidates are on the table: KH growth ([Rodríguez 2023](../notes/rodriguez-2023-preferred-flapping-round-twin-jets.md)), guided-mode existence bands ([Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md); [Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md)), and loop gain between competing global modes ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 29; [Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md), p. 26). Bands bound the tones but admit both SS and SA. Bell's 5–15 % split between the symmetric and antisymmetric tones, with different wavelengths, hints that each symmetry closes its own loop (the note's inference). *To settle:* a complex-valued (gain-bearing) resonance model or Floquet-in-x/global analysis of twin-jet mean flows, as suggested in [Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md) (p. 22), tested against cross-plane PIV that can separate 1SS from 1AS ([Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md), p. 21). A cheap first test is Nogueira's Floquet LSA on Bell's NPR 4.6 mean flow: do the 1SS and 1SA guided-mode bands both reach St 0.22–0.23?
- **Is intermittency intrinsic or driven?** Candidates are stochastic switching between competing global modes, a reading that starts with [Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md) (p. 29) and that [Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md) test against supply drift (p. 39–41); frequency detuning between nominally identical nozzles ([Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 10–11); and AA/AS competition ([Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md)). A fourth comes from re-reading Bell's statistics: the wide states have envelope spreads of 5.5–6 dB, close to the 5.6 dB of narrow-band Gaussian noise, and interruption fractions that track the threshold as noise would, which favours weakly damped, noise-driven modes over switching between saturated limit cycles (the note's analysis); Bell tested neither supply drift nor beating. The two OSU papers disagree at M_j 1.35: one reports beating with a drifting phase, the other two tones 150 Hz apart, with in-phase coupling interrupted at about 123 Hz and no phase drift ([Esfahani 2021, control](../notes/esfahani-2021-control-twin-rectangular-jet-coupling.md), p. 10). *To settle:* long records with logged plenum pressure and temperature, per-jet and per-symmetry Hilbert tracking, the beat test (beat frequency equal to the frequency difference), and a Rayleigh null for every envelope statistic.
- **Which wave closes the cross-excitation loop?** [Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md) say the guided mode; [Esfahani 2021, control](../notes/esfahani-2021-control-twin-rectangular-jet-coupling.md) frame it as free-stream acoustics (p. 11). Guided-mode bands have been computed for a single rectangular jet ([Nogueira 2025](../notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4219) but not yet for rectangular twins.
- **Lateral coupling.** [Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md) see none in the major-axis plane (p. 9), but the companion paper's regime map lists in-phase coupling in both planes when the jets are underexpanded ([Esfahani 2021, control](../notes/esfahani-2021-control-twin-rectangular-jet-coupling.md), p. 9).

## Methods and data
- **Time-resolved data** (high-speed schlieren, LES, microphones) supports D2-split SPOD ([Yeung 2022](../notes/yeung-2022-3d-spod-twin-rectangular-jet.md)); CWT of POD coefficients and conditional sampling ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md)); coherence at all frequencies to find coexisting tones, then band-pass Hilbert envelopes and phases per tone ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 8–14), which need a stated filter, a beat test and a Rayleigh null (the note's analysis); wavelet coherence between microphone pairs, one pair across a jet for its mode and one across the jets for coupling ([Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 5–9); and Hilbert phase difference and two-probe gain maps ([Jeun 2024](../notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md)). The frequency shifts between A1/A2 states are 0.3–0.6 %, so SPOD needs long blocks and Morlet wavelets need hundreds of cycles ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md)); the m = 1 tones are 5–15 % apart and much easier to separate ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md)).
- **Non-time-resolved data** (PIV, 1 Hz schlieren) supports symmetry-imposed POD pairs followed by k_x filtering and LSA ([Nogueira 2021](../notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md)); sub-domain and conditional POD on each jet's pair amplitude ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 22–27); two-point correlation of schlieren ([Knast 2018](../notes/knast-2018-coupling-modes-underexpanded-twin-axisymmetric-jet.md)); and log σ maps for standing waves ([Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 15–17). POD pairs fail when the coupling switches ([Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md)).

## Relevance to our work
- **Group rigs.** The group's twin round nozzle plates ([Knast 2018](../notes/knast-2018-coupling-modes-underexpanded-twin-axisymmetric-jet.md); [Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md); [Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md); [Wong 2023](../notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md)) are the data this page is about. Fitted resonance parameters differ between rigs, and lip thickness (15 % against 3 %) is the suspected cause ([Stavropoulos 2023](../notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md), p. 16). Identical nozzles on two Monash rigs also give different state NPRs, because of the enclosure walls and the plenum face ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md), p. 6, 17–18). Record lip, flange, enclosure and upstream-surface geometry.
- **SPOD → reconstruction → HPOD.** The pipeline fits the open intermittency questions. Reconstruct the tone band separately from even and odd fields (or D2 components), and from each half-domain, then track instantaneous amplitude and frequency per symmetry and per jet. That replaces Bell's assumption of one microphone per jet with direct envelopes and gives the inter-jet phase that conditional POD can only infer (the Bell note). Choose band widths with the 0.3–0.6 % (A1/A2) or 5–15 % (m = 1) shifts in mind, test for beats before calling anything switching, and compare envelope statistics with a Rayleigh null. First target: round twins at s/D 3, NPR 3.4–4.4 ([Bell 2021](../notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md)).
- **PyFR staging LES.** If envelope statistics near a staging boundary look Rayleigh-like, switching is noise-driven, and dwell times follow escape-rate statistics rather than the deterministic 1/t_switch fit of the continuation driver (the Bell note's suggestion; see [screech-staging-and-mode-switching](screech-staging-and-mode-switching.md)).
- **Rocket noise.** Screech is absent at full scale ([Lubert 2022](../notes/lubert-2022-supersonic-jet-noise-launch-vehicles-review.md)). Coupling still sets near-field loads in the lab: about 15 dB between coupling modes midway between the jets ([Raman 1998](../notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 90–91). The "equivalent geometry" of merging jets ([Stavropoulos 2024](../notes/stavropoulos-2024-upstream-waves-merging-twin-jets.md)) parallels the merged equivalent jet in Eldred-type clustered-nozzle models ([Kandula 2008](../notes/kandula-2008-near-field-acoustics-clustered-rocket-engines.md)); the link is mine.
