# Non-axisymmetric jets: rectangular and elliptical

_Last updated: 2026-10-24 · 23 papers_

## Scope
Single supersonic, mostly screeching, jets from rectangular and elliptical nozzles: the oscillation shapes they select, symmetry decompositions on single jets, passive control by nozzle shape, and how linear models (vortex sheets, Floquet-in-azimuth stability) and LES of single non-round jets compare with experiment. Round jets whose flapping axis is fixed by weak streaks pose the same symmetry-breaking problem and are included.

Axis switching and the evolving mean flow (where the switch sits, streamwise and corner vortices, the bi-axial and quasi-helical modes across the switch, each tone's own mean flow) are on [axis-switching-and-mean-flow](axis-switching-and-mean-flow.md). Twin jets, with the full D2 (SS/SA/AS/AA) treatment and the table mapping each paper's labels, are on [twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md). The screech loop is on [screech](screech.md) and staging and mode switching on [screech-staging-and-mode-switching](screech-staging-and-mode-switching.md); this page says only what changes with geometry. See also [linear-models](linear-models.md) (local stability, vortex sheets, Floquet models, guided-jet waves), [linear-operator-methods](linear-operator-methods.md) (resolvent, global stability, OWNS), [shock-cell-models](shock-cell-models.md) (shock-cell solutions), [modal-decomposition](modal-decomposition.md) (SPOD/POD practice), [time-frequency-methods](time-frequency-methods.md) (Hilbert and wavelet tracking), [impinging-jets-and-edge-tones](impinging-jets-and-edge-tones.md) (non-axisymmetric impinging jets) and [jet-noise-mechanisms](jet-noise-mechanisms.md) (mixing noise, BBSAN).

**Naming.** *Flapping* is the transverse oscillation across the minor axis (Mathieu mode se₁ of an elliptic jet), *wagging* the same motion in the major-axis plane (ce₁), *varicose* ce₀ ([Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md)). In the single-jet D2 labels of [Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md) (first letter about y = 0, second about z = 0), AS = flapping and SA = wagging, and the diagonal flapping of [Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md) (p. 21–24) is AA by the note's mapping. Twin-jet papers use the same letters for other planes (table on [twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md)).

## Current understanding
1. **Breaking axisymmetry turns helices into flapping.** In a round jet the m = ±1 modes are degenerate, so flapping and helical motions are equivalent. Any π-periodic distortion of the cross-section (an ellipse, a second jet, weak |m| = 2 streaks) splits them into a flapping and a wagging eigenmode with different growth rates, and one wins ([Rodríguez 2023](../Notes/rodriguez-2023-preferred-flapping-round-twin-jets.md), p. 8–12; [Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 7; [Nogueira 2026](../Notes/nogueira-2026-flapping-motion-screeching-round-jets.md), p. 7–10). Experiments, LES and DNS agree that minor-axis flapping dominates rectangular and elliptical jets ([Krothapalli 1986](../Notes/krothapalli-1986-screech-tones-mixing-underexpanded-rectangular-jet.md), p. 138–139; [Raman 1998](../Notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 54; [Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md), p. 131–132, 140; [Mitchell 2013](../Notes/mitchell-2013-near-field-structure-underexpanded-elliptic-jets.md), p. 7–10; [Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md), p. 2743–2746; [Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 15; [Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4214, 4217; [Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 10). The exception is cold LES of an AR 2 C-D nozzle, which flaps along the diagonal until it is heated ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 21–24).
2. **AR 2 jets stage and are multimodal; AR ≥ 4 jets mostly switch only once.** AR 2 jets switch from varicose to flapping near NPR 2.1–2.2, then stage by shock peak (k_s1 → k_s2) without changing symmetry, and run weaker varicose, second-flapping and wagging loops beside the flapping tone ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 1009–1017; [Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 18; [Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md), p. 7, 15–17; [Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4214–4215; [Lu 2026](../Notes/lu-2026-rectangular-jet-screech-dimensional-reduction.md), p. 20). At AR 4, strong flapping breaks up the shock cells before a suboptimal peak can form, so after the varicose → flapping switch there are no further stages (Edgington-Mitchell 2022, p. 18–19). **Caveat:** thick-lipped rectangular nozzles are reported to stage and carry simultaneous tones ([Edgington-Mitchell 2019](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2019-aeroacoustic-resonance-review.md), p. 133), but the cases in the library show a second symmetry or a stronger single mode, not new stages (Staging below).
3. **The mean flow evolves with the oscillation.** Axis switching follows the large-scale oscillation, and the switched jet supports modes the upstream jet does not ([Krothapalli 1986](../Notes/krothapalli-1986-screech-tones-mixing-underexpanded-rectangular-jet.md); [Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md); [Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md); details on [axis-switching-and-mean-flow](axis-switching-and-mean-flow.md)).
4. **The upstream guided jet mode closes the loop.** Its existence band explains symmetry selection better than KH growth does ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 25–27; [Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 22–24), as in twin jets. A planar model is good enough for the KH wave from AR 2 up, but its guided-mode band is far too wide and admits tones that never appear, so the band edges need the real cross-section and mean flow ([Lu 2026](../Notes/lu-2026-rectangular-jet-screech-dimensional-reduction.md), p. 9–12, 17, 20). On a measured mean flow, a weakest-link model predicts the flapping tone of an AR 2 rectangular jet within a few per cent ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4218–4220). Nothing yet predicts which flapping plane or coexisting loop wins, which tones exist, or the amplitude.
5. **A single view or average misleads** (see symmetry decompositions and methods below): views hide or fake symmetries, mirrored phase-locked cycles impose them, and a mean or a whole-record transform blurs alternating states.

## Mode shapes: flapping, wagging, varicose, helical
[Krothapalli 1986](../Notes/krothapalli-1986-screech-tones-mixing-underexpanded-rectangular-jet.md) gave the classic evidence: in an AR 16.7 jet, phase-locked schlieren show the two shear layers exactly out of phase (p. 138–139), with minor-axis flapping near x/D ≈ 10 and a major-axis oscillation beyond 60 D, each at St ≈ 0.12 on its own dimension, read from one station and one peak through Crighton's elliptic vortex sheet (p. 139–140).

In [Raman 1998](../Notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md)'s pre-2000 picture, rectangular jets are antisymmetric; symmetric modes appear near choking (Gutmark et al. saw a switch at M_j 1.15) but stay weak, their feedback efficiency being low (p. 54–55; [Raman 1999](../Notes/raman-1999-supersonic-jet-screech-half-century-powell-present.md), p. 548–549). Phase-locked stereo PIV shows an AR 4 C-D jet flapping over the whole sweep, M_j 1.2–1.8, with only the flapping frequency in the near field ([Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md), p. 131–132, 140). Flapping does not need screech: an ideally expanded multi-stream rectangular jet has flapping POD modes at St 0.46–0.70 ([Berry 2017](../Notes/berry-2017-pod-time-resolved-schlieren-multi-stream-rectangular-jet.md), p. 8).

[Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md) rotate the nozzle through 19 views: the screech SPOD eigenvalue λ_s(θ) is flat for varicose modes and v-shaped, lowest in the major-axis view, for flapping (p. 9–11). In the group's thick-lipped AR 2 ellipse, two-plane PIV first showed flapping as broad centreline v_rms peaks at the shock spacing and a bimodal centreline v PDF, both in the minor-axis plane only ([Mitchell 2013](../Notes/mitchell-2013-near-field-structure-underexpanded-elliptic-jets.md), p. 7–10). It grows with NPR (p. 9), as does the minor-axis POD pair: 15 % of the transverse-velocity energy at NPR 2.2 ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md), p. 2745–2746), 23.6 % at 2.6 and 35 % at 3.4, against 6.8 % and 10.3 % on the major axis ([Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 15). Two-view schlieren SPOD of a sharp-throat AR 2 C-D rectangular jet (the OSU twin design at about 0.45 scale) finds four tones at NPR 4.0: minor-axis flapping (St 0.265), varicose (0.337, present from NPR 2.7), a second flapping tone (0.308) and wagging seen only across the major axis (0.272), the last two only near and above ideal expansion; the far field and the PIV POD register only the first ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4214–4217). In forced subsonic DNS, AS flapping dominates at every forcing level and SA wagging decays faster ([Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 8, 10).

**The nozzle alone does not fix the flapping plane.** LES of the Cincinnati AR 2 C-D nozzle at NPR 3 flaps along the diagonal when cold (AA, with 180° phase jumps across both planes), is mixed at TR 2 and flaps purely across the minor axis at TR 2.5 and 3 ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 21–24), concentrating the hot jets' Mach-wave radiation in that plane (p. 16). One DFT over the record cannot say whether the diagonal pattern is steady or an average of alternating AS and AA states (the note's reading); the twin version of this nozzle does alternate ([Jeun 2024](../Notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md), p. 9–10). The major-axis-view signature of the dominant tone in [Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), put down by the authors to misalignment or poor convergence (p. 4214), could be the same diagonal component (the note's suggestion).

**Why flapping wins.** In a single round jet the m = 1 eigenvalue appears twice, in AS and SA, and a helix is SA1 + i AS1; a second jet splits the two into four eigenvalues whose small differences in k_i grow exponentially with x, so a helix at s/D 2.2 becomes SA1 flapping within a few diameters ([Rodríguez 2023](../Notes/rodriguez-2023-preferred-flapping-round-twin-jets.md), p. 8–9, 12). [Nogueira 2026](../Notes/nogueira-2026-flapping-motion-screeching-round-jets.md) apply the same N = 2 Floquet argument to a single round jet: a |m| = 2 streak of amplitude 0.01 already fixes the flapping axis (p. 10, 12), and in a heated M_j 1.35 LES the flapping plane precesses with slowly rotating streaks, drifting about π/2 over about 100 screech cycles (p. 20–24). An ellipse supplies the N = 2 periodicity itself ([Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 7), and so does a convex impingement cylinder, which replaces a round jet's helical modes with flapping locked normal to its axis ([Edgington-Mitchell 2019](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2019-aeroacoustic-resonance-review.md), p. 148). Treating ellipses, twin jets and streaky round jets as one symmetry-breaking problem is my reading; each paper treats only its own case.

**Helical-looking motion** rests on weak evidence. The helical-like POD pair of the AR 2 ellipse ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md)) and the quasi-helical mode of [Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md) grow at or beyond the axis switch, and neither has been tested across planes; both are discussed with the switch on [axis-switching-and-mean-flow](axis-switching-and-mean-flow.md). Mode V of the thick-lipped ellipse (NPR 3.95–4.3) is the one candidate that dominates the spectrum, but it is read as helical only from weak correlations in two views taken at different times ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 1017, 1021–1022), and PIV of the same nozzle at NPR 4.2 shows no flapping before x/d ≈ 6 and a vortex bifurcation instead ([Mitchell 2013](../Notes/mitchell-2013-near-field-structure-underexpanded-elliptic-jets.md), p. 9, 11–12; open question below). The "helical" coupling once reported for round twins has been recast as a mixture of coupling states ([twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md)).

## Staging and multiple tones
Only what changes with geometry; the mechanisms are on [screech-staging-and-mode-switching](screech-staging-and-mode-switching.md).

**AR 2.** The thick-lipped ellipse runs five stages over NPR 1.95–4.3: varicose I (St ≈ 1) to NPR 2.05, flapping II, III and IV from 2.10, 3.15 and 3.40, and mode V from 3.95 ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), Fig. 2, p. 1009–1010). The thin-lipped ellipse and the AR 2 rectangular jet switch to flapping about 0.1 NPR later, near 2.2 ([Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md), p. 7). II → III keeps the symmetry, but shock and standing-wave spacings, equal within 5 % in II and IV, differ by about 15 % in III (p. 1020–1021): the loop has moved from k_s1 to the suboptimal peak k_s2 ([Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 18), which the authors then read as Shen & Tam's waveguide feedback (p. 1020). Only the dominant tone was mapped, so coexistence and hysteresis are untested (the note's reading). The AR 2 rectangular jet adds a k_s2 band from NPR ≈ 3.5 ([Lu 2026](../Notes/lu-2026-rectangular-jet-screech-dimensional-reduction.md), p. 20).

**AR ≥ 4.** Most rectangular jets switch from varicose near choking to flapping and then hold it ([Edgington-Mitchell 2019](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2019-aeroacoustic-resonance-review.md), p. 133). At AR 4, flapping displaces the shocks by up to half a jet width by the third cell, the shock spectrum has one broad peak, and without k_s2 there is no second loop; reports show no staging at AR ≥ 4 and one stage change at AR 3 (Edgington-Mitchell 2022, p. 18–19). The AR 4 C-D jet of [Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md) fits: its Strouhal jump at the design Mach number, usually read as varicose → flapping but put down by the authors to U_c falling from 0.63 to 0.50 U_j (p. 132), crosses a toneless gap at M_j 1.42–1.46 rather than a hysteretic change of stage (the note's reading); no overexpanded PIV is shown, and the mirrored cycle imposes the antisymmetry being measured (p. 130).

**Lip and exit geometry.** The 2019 review adds that thicker lips bring staging and simultaneous tones (p. 133), citing Walker et al. 1997 and Thethy et al. 2017, neither in the library. What the library holds is weaker: Walker's AR 7 2-D C-D jet runs a symmetric and an antisymmetric tone together ([Raman 1998](../Notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 56; [Shen 2002](../Notes/shen-2002-three-dimensional-numerical-simulation-jet-screech.md), p. 38), a second symmetry rather than a k_s2 stage (my reading), and thick lips on Raman's own rectangular jets strengthened their single flapping mode (Passive control below). Bevelled AR 5 exits carry tones fitted by several spanwise waveguide modes (Raman 1998, p. 81–87; [Raman 1999](../Notes/raman-1999-supersonic-jet-screech-half-century-powell-present.md), p. 560). With a plate, an AR 4.24 jet runs varicose and flapping loops at once, each of its 20+ tones a combination of the two ([Tam & Norum 1992](../Notes/tam-1992-impingement-tones-large-aspect-ratio-rectangular-jets.md), p. 305–308), but there the plate closes the loop ([impinging-jets-and-edge-tones](impinging-jets-and-edge-tones.md)).

## Axis switching and the evolving mean flow
Rectangular and elliptical jets switch axes downstream, and the switch follows the oscillation: screech or forcing pulls it upstream, ideally expanded or unforced jets switch late or not at all, and heating pushes it downstream in LES ([Krothapalli 1986](../Notes/krothapalli-1986-screech-tones-mixing-underexpanded-rectangular-jet.md); [Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md); [Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md); [Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md)). In the AR 2 jets it lies within the first few shock cells, with wagging or "new flapping" structures beyond it ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md); [Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md); [Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md)), and each of two mutually exclusive tones of an AR 2 ellipse holds its own mean flow ([Mazharmanesh 2026](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2026-triadic-interaction-mean-flow-deformation-screech.md)). Mechanisms and open questions are on [axis-switching-and-mean-flow](axis-switching-and-mean-flow.md).

## Symmetry decompositions for single jets
The general machinery, built mostly on twin jets, is on [twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md): the exact, orthogonal D2 split that cuts 3-D SPOD memory 4× ([Yeung 2022](../Notes/yeung-2022-3d-spod-twin-rectangular-jet.md)), symmetry-imposed POD of planar PIV, and sub-domain, conditional and half-domain POD. For single jets:
- **D2 and even–odd splits.** Applied before pressure SPOD of DNS, with zero-weight sub-domains, the D2 split separates the wagging and new-flapping structures ([Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 5, 14). Even and odd PIV fields slightly improve POD convergence for an ellipse ([Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 15).
- **Path-integrated and planar views.** Two orthogonal views give a symmetry fingerprint (flapping only in the minor-axis view, wagging only in the major-axis view, varicose in both), but an antisymmetric mode need not vanish from the orthogonal view, as the sharp-throat jet's dominant tone shows (Mode shapes above; [Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4214). In an elliptic jet the barrel shock shows only in the major-axis view, and minor-axis expansion-wave reflections look like shocks there ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md), p. 2741–2742). PIV sees the Mach stem's wake (from NPR 2.6; in a round jet only at 4.2) only in the major-axis plane, probably through out-of-plane averaging over the ≈ 0.1d measurement depth ([Mitchell 2013](../Notes/mitchell-2013-near-field-structure-underexpanded-elliptic-jets.md), p. 9–11), and per-plane centreline transverse-velocity PDFs classify flapping without phase data (p. 7–10). The mean image of a flapping jet looks artificially thick ([Samimy 1998](../Notes/samimy-1998-rectangular-jet-trailing-edge-control.md), p. 7).
- **Nozzle rotation.** Comparing modes from mirror-image views with an inner product β stands in for a D2 split ([Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md), p. 8–9).

## Linear models against experiment and LES
**Shock cells and frequency.** [Tam 1988](../Notes/tam-1988-shock-cell-screech-rectangular-non-axisymmetric-jets.md) extends the Prandtl–Pack vortex sheet to any cross-section (shock-cell models in general are on [shock-cell-models](shock-cell-models.md)). The minor dimension sets the spacing, the aspect-ratio correction is under 3 % for AR > 4 (p. 6), measured spacing for M_j 1.1–1.7 agrees (p. 9–10), and the screech formula with u_c = 0.7 u_j sits slightly above the data, untested below AR 4 (p. 12). LES of an AR 2 C-D jet ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md)) fills that gap: Tam's first-cell length is within 1–5 %, the cells shrink linearly downstream (mean ΔL/L_s 5.5 %, as in round jets), heating cuts the visible count from 10 to 4 (p. 15), and the screech formula with the LES u_c is within about 10 % (p. 19). In the AR 4 jet, Powell's formula with the measured U_c and L_s ≈ 3.2h reproduces St ≈ 0.09 to about 1 %, while Tam's spacing (≈ 3.0h) is about 6 % short (the Alkislar note's check). Measured convection speeds sit below 0.7 u_j: about 0.5 u_j, vorticity-weighted and constant in x, in the AR 4 jet ([Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md), p. 143–144), and 0.60–0.67 u_j when Tam's formula is inverted for the AR 2 LES tones (the Gojon note's arithmetic). u_c = 0.55 u_j fits AR 3–16.7 data better, and finite shear-layer thickness shortens the spacing by about 20 % ([Raman 1998](../Notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 59, 62; [Raman 1999](../Notes/raman-1999-supersonic-jet-screech-half-century-powell-present.md), p. 551–552). In the AR 2 ellipse, Powell's formula with the measured spacing is within 8 % in flapping modes II and IV but 14 % low in III, where the loop uses k_s2 ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), Table 4, p. 1021; U_c ≈ 0.65 U_j as reconstructed in the note). The area-equivalent diameter collapses screech St for AR 2–5 but fails for AR ≥ 9.6 ([Esfahani 2021, coupling modes](../Notes/esfahani-2021-coupling-modes-twin-rectangular-jets.md), p. 7–8). On the small dimension, St at peak screech is 0.11–0.14 for rectangular jets against 0.24–0.28 for round ones ([Krothapalli 1986](../Notes/krothapalli-1986-screech-tones-mixing-underexpanded-rectangular-jet.md), p. 140). The nozzle barely moves the AR 2 tone: at M_j ≈ 1.55 the cold Cincinnati LES (St 0.24–0.25), the sharp-throat jet (0.265), the OSU twins (0.27) and the group's convergent AR 2 rectangular jet (flapping at 0.23, NPR 4.0) agree within about 0.04 (the Gojon and Nogueira notes' comparisons; [Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md), p. 15).

**KH and guided modes on measured mean flows.**
- *Elliptical jet, Floquet LSA* ([Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md)). On fitted PIV and RANS mean flows the se₁ KH wavenumbers fall within the POD range, whichever mean flow is used (p. 18–20), and the se₁ guided-mode centreline peak matches POD "almost perfectly" (p. 21–22). The guided-mode band is at most 0.09 wide in St and shifts with shear-layer thickness, so RANS gives narrower bands (p. 22–23). At the screech St the mode is neutral only over a short streamwise stretch, where the measured guided-mode amplitude peaks (p. 22–24).
- *Rectangular jet, planar vortex sheet* ([Tam & Norum 1992](../Notes/tam-1992-impingement-tones-large-aspect-ratio-rectangular-jets.md); [Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md)). Tam & Norum built these upstream-neutral-mode bands for impinging AR 4.24 jets, the first symmetric (S1) band lying wholly below the first antisymmetric (A1), their reason the varicose tone is the lower (p. 309–310). The ordering is not general: one of their own pairs is closer than first bands allow (a/s = 1.26 against about 1.4; the note's check), and the free sharp-throat AR 2 jet has its varicose tone above its flapping one (St 0.337 against 0.265; [Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4215). The NPR 3 LES tones of Gojon et al. fall only in the A1 band, which the authors take as the reason for flapping, and the LES pressure shows upstream energy near that band's lower limit (p. 24–27). They place cold AR 4–5 experimental tones, including [Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md)'s, in or just below the band, and blame those below it on the infinitely thin vortex sheet (p. 26). The model ignores the major dimension, so it cannot separate minor-axis from diagonal flapping. By the note's arithmetic the hot underexpanded tones fall 8 % and 31 % below the band, between the S1 and A1 bands, as by eye do many cold points above M_j ≈ 1.45, so band membership is a weaker test of closure than the authors suggest (my reading). The lower edge the argument leans on is also the one a planar model gets most wrong ([Lu 2026](../Notes/lu-2026-rectangular-jet-screech-dimensional-reduction.md), next bullet).
- *Rectangular jet, Floquet LSA* ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md)). The k_x spectrum of the PIV flapping mode has a subsonic upstream peak near k_KH − k_sh1, so the dominant loop closes on the first shock peak (p. 4218). On a fitted shock-free 3-D mean flow, a weakest-link model (α_GJM = α_KH − k_sh) predicts the flapping tone within a few per cent at all six PIV NPRs, near the POD-mode peak (p. 4219–4220); a constant-profile base flow works only at low NPR, so the spreading must be modelled (p. 4220). The k_sh2 loop offered for the second flapping tone sits as close to the varicose tone on frequency alone (the note's reading), and the varicose and wagging tones are not modelled.
- *Rectangular jet, 2-D cross-plane LSA* ([Lu 2026](../Notes/lu-2026-rectangular-jet-screech-dimensional-reduction.md)). On a shock-free tanh base flow for AR 1–8, a planar model gets the flapping KH wave right: wavenumber errors of at most 7.8 % at AR 2 (0.3 % for St_h ≥ 0.2) and 3.3 % at AR 4, growth-rate error 6.2 % at AR 2, against over 30 % for a square jet (p. 9–12, 21), so Tam's AR ≥ 4 planar rule extends from shock cells to KH waves (p. 12). An m = 1 round model is worse even for a square jet (p. 14). The planar guided-mode band is much too wide: its upper edge (saddle) is within 3.4 % for AR ≥ 4, but its lower edge (branch point) is badly off even at AR 8, and at AR 2 the 2-D band lies wholly above it (p. 17). On fitted PIV mean flows ("a square with rounded corners" at AR 2) the band narrows to ΔSt_h ≈ 0.02–0.04 and moves down (p. 18–19), as on Ivelja's mean flows. Planar weakest-link loops follow the dominant tone and, on k_s2, the second band, but predict many tones that do not exist (p. 20). The authors say the 2-D PIV model captures the tones too; by the note's reading of Fig. 24 it misses the dominant tone at NPR 3.3 and 3.7, and its base flow seems to come from the sharp-throat C-D nozzle, the tones from the convergent one (the note's inference).
- *Streaky round jet, Floquet LSA* ([Nogueira 2026](../Notes/nogueira-2026-flapping-motion-screeching-round-jets.md)). The guided-mode band moves up in frequency for flapping and down for wagging (p. 11).

**Cross-plane resolvent.** Resolvent streaks and corner vortices in rectangular jets ([Marshall 2024](../Notes/marshall-2024-prediction-streaks-rectangular-jets.md)) are on [axis-switching-and-mean-flow](axis-switching-and-mean-flow.md), and the method on [linear-operator-methods](linear-operator-methods.md).

**LES.** Implicit LES of the single Cincinnati nozzle matches the measured tone within 0.01 in St at six conditions, along with the PIV mean flow and the OASPL directivity, but its screech level rises by 26 dB from TR 1 to 3 while the measured level falls ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 18–19, 21–22, 24). Twin-jet LES is assessed on [twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md).

**Verdict.** Local models get wavenumbers and mode shapes right, and a planar model gets the KH wave cheaply from AR 2 up. Guided-mode band edges need the real cross-section and mean flow, so planar bands bound the tones only loosely and admit tones that never appear; on a measured mean flow the weakest-link model predicts the dominant tone, but no model yet says which tones exist. LES still gets the amplitude trend with temperature wrong, and all the models are locally parallel.

## Passive control by nozzle shape
Trailing-edge cutouts on AR 3 Mach 2 nozzles make streamwise vortices only off-design, raising mixing by up to about 50 % ([Samimy 1998](../Notes/samimy-1998-rectangular-jet-trailing-edge-control.md), p. 7–8). They lower noise at 30° by up to 12 dB when the jet is underexpanded ([Kerechanin 2001](../Notes/kerechanin-2001-trailing-edge-rectangular-jet-acoustics.md), p. 3). On overexpanded jets, single-sided cuts remove screech, while symmetric two-sided cuts amplify it and raise its frequency, which the authors attribute to breaking or keeping the symmetry of the shock cells about the major axis (p. 4–5). A thick lip revives weak screech ([Samimy 1998](../Notes/samimy-1998-rectangular-jet-trailing-edge-control.md), p. 8). On Raman's rectangular jets a thicker lip strengthens the oscillation up to t/h ≈ 1, and at M_j 1.75 a t/h 2 lip restores screech that had stopped, by 23 dB, with no second mode reported ([Raman 1998](../Notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 75–77; [Raman 1999](../Notes/raman-1999-supersonic-jet-screech-half-century-powell-present.md), p. 557–558).

## Twin jets
A second jet breaks axisymmetry as an ellipse does and adds coupling: steady symmetric or antisymmetric coupling, switching, one jet oscillating alone and uncoupling all occur ([Wong 2023](../Notes/wong-2023-steady-unsteady-coupling-twin-round-jets.md); [Bell 2021](../Notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md); [Jeun 2024](../Notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md)), symmetric coupling sits slightly lower in frequency as stability models predict ([Stavropoulos 2023](../Notes/stavropoulos-2023-axisymmetric-screech-round-twin-jets-lsa.md)), and each jet flaps as a single jet would. All of this is on [twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md).

## Disagreements and open questions
- **What fixes the flapping plane of a single jet?** For the cold diagonal flapping that heating turns minor-axis ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md)), candidates are heating shifting the guided-mode bands, a time average of competing AS and AA states as in the twin jets ([Jeun 2024](../Notes/jeun-2024-screech-coupling-closure-rectangular-twin-jets.md)), and streaks pinning or slowly rotating the plane as in round jets ([Nogueira 2026](../Notes/nogueira-2026-flapping-motion-screeching-round-jets.md), p. 20–24). The planar band model cannot tell AS from AA. *To settle:* D2-split SPOD of the LES at the tone with per-symmetry Hilbert tracking, and rotated-view schlieren of a cold C-D jet, which may already show a diagonal component ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md)).
- **Do the multimodal AR 2 jets' loops coexist or alternate?** The four tones of the sharp-throat jet come from block-averaged SPOD, so whether they run together or take turns is untested ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md)); in the AR 2 ellipse two flapping tones are mutually exclusive ([Mazharmanesh 2026](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2026-triadic-interaction-mean-flow-deformation-screech.md)). *To settle:* per-tone Hilbert tracking of time-resolved schlieren (Relevance below).
- **Do thick lips make AR ≥ 4 jets stage?** Shock breakdown leaves an AR 4 jet no k_s2 ([Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 18–19), yet the 2019 review reports staging with thick lips (p. 133). The library's cases show a second symmetry (the AR 7 jet) or a stronger single mode (Raman's), not a new stage, and a thick lip may raise receptivity enough to sustain a weaker loop without restoring k_s2 (the 2019 note's reading). *To settle:* index Walker et al. 1997 and Thethy et al. 2017, then take time-resolved schlieren of one AR ≥ 4 nozzle with thin and thick lips: a k_s2 shock peak with a tone at k_kh − k_s2 means shock-peak staging, a varicose tone beside the flapping one means coexisting symmetries.
- **What screeches in the AR 2 ellipse above NPR 3.95?** Mode V is read as helical from weak antisymmetric correlations (|R| ≲ 0.3, the note's reading), the authors arguing that strong underexpansion deforms the jet away from an ellipse and unlocks the mode from the axes ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 1017, 1021–1022). At NPR 4.2 the same nozzle's centreline v_rms is if anything larger in the major-axis plane (the note's reading of Fig. 13), with a vortex bifurcation that may suppress flapping ([Mitchell 2013](../Notes/mitchell-2013-near-field-structure-underexpanded-elliptic-jets.md), p. 9, 12). The 2019 review doubts a helical mode can form near enough to the nozzle to screech (p. 134), yet a thin-lipped ellipse has a quasi-helical mode at NPR 3.6 ([Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md), p. 16–19). *To settle:* rotated-view SPOD of the thick-lipped nozzle at NPR 4.0–4.3 (λ_s flat in θ for a helix), with Hilbert tracking to tell a helix from alternating flapping and wagging.
- **Which tones exist?** Planar guided-mode bands admit many tones never observed ([Lu 2026](../Notes/lu-2026-rectangular-jet-screech-dimensional-reduction.md), p. 20). The 2-D bands on PIV mean flows are narrow but, by the note's reading, miss the dominant tone at NPR 3.3 and 3.7, and the base flow and the tones probably come from different nozzles. *To settle:* 2-D LSA on PIV mean flows of the nozzle whose tones are compared, checked against tone onset and loss in a fine NPR sweep.
- **What sets the tone amplitude?** The authors of the Cincinnati LES explain its wrong level trend only by an initially disturbed LES jet against a fully turbulent experiment ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 18–19, 27). [Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md) argue that coherent circulation at the end of the third shock cell, with shock strength, sets the amplitude (p. 142–144, 153), and the AR 2 ellipse also produces its tone where coherent vorticity peaks before the third shock ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md), p. 2747). But Alkislar's evidence is one phase at two conditions, and the quieter jet is only 1–3 dB quieter with less than half the circulation (the note's reading). In 2-D jets the third cell emits single feedback shocks, absent in round and low-AR rectangular jets, and the source moves to the fourth shock as M_j rises from 1.45 to 1.75, while beyond a threshold stronger shocks do not raise the amplitude ([Raman 1999](../Notes/raman-1999-supersonic-jet-screech-half-century-powell-present.md), p. 552–557). *To settle:* Hilbert-tracked coherent amplitude at the radiating cell against the microphone envelope, and LES with matched exit turbulence at each TR.
- Three questions about the switch (are bi-axial and quasi-helical modes one wavepacket or two; does screech drive the switch or the switched mean flow select the mode; what are corner vortices) are on [axis-switching-and-mean-flow](axis-switching-and-mean-flow.md). Coupling questions (what selects the symmetry, whether intermittency is intrinsic, which wave closes the cross-excitation loop) are on [twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md).

## Methods and data
- **Time-resolved data** (high-speed schlieren, LES, DNS, microphones): SPOD with nozzle rotation and sub-domains ([Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md)) or with two views, plus a k–ω dispersion relation along one line that shows the KH, guided and duct branches ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4213–4214); a Fourier transform of the 3-D LES field at the tone and k–ω spectra inside the jet for upstream waves ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 21–27), which cannot tell a steady pattern from alternating states (the note's reading); D2-split SPOD with sub-domains ([Suzuki 2026](../Notes/suzuki-2026-coherent-structures-axis-switching-elliptical-jets.md)); BMD and short-time means that tie each tone to its own mean flow ([Mazharmanesh 2026](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2026-triadic-interaction-mean-flow-deformation-screech.md)).
- **Non-time-resolved data** (PIV, 1 Hz schlieren): symmetry-imposed POD pairs, k_x filtering and LSA ([Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md)) on a fitted shock-free 3-D mean flow ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), p. 4215–4216); microphone-triggered phase-locked stereo PIV when the tone is clean, without mirroring half the cycle ([Alkislar 2003](../Notes/alkislar-2003-screeching-rectangular-jet-stereo-piv.md), p. 130); strobe schlieren locked to one band-filtered tone, which isolates one loop when two run ([Tam & Norum 1992](../Notes/tam-1992-impingement-tones-large-aspect-ratio-rectangular-jets.md), p. 305); two-point correlation of schlieren, also giving vortex and standing-wave spacings ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md), p. 2743; [Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md), p. 1012–1020); per-plane centreline v PDFs ([Mitchell 2013](../Notes/mitchell-2013-near-field-structure-underexpanded-elliptic-jets.md), p. 7–10).
- **Aliasing.** Check the tone against Nyquist when snapshot POD runs on decimated schlieren ([Berry 2017](../Notes/berry-2017-pod-time-resolved-schlieren-multi-stream-rectangular-jet.md), p. 8).

## Relevance to our work
- **Group rigs.** The AR 2 elliptical nozzles ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-multimodal-instability-weakly-underexpanded-elliptic-jet.md); [Ivelja 2024](../Notes/ivelja-2024-modal-analysis-screeching-elliptical-jets.md); [Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md)) and the AR 2 convergent and sharp-throat C-D rectangular nozzles ([Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md); [Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md)) are the data this page is about; the twin round nozzle plates are on [twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md). The thick- and thin-lipped ellipses switch to flapping about 0.1 NPR apart ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md); [Mitchell 2013](../Notes/mitchell-2013-near-field-structure-underexpanded-elliptic-jets.md) uses the same thick-lipped nozzle), so record lip thickness, and record nozzle orientation against camera and microphones: the view decides which symmetries a schlieren sees, and a flapping mode locked to one axis makes screech directivity strongly azimuthal ([Edgington-Mitchell 2019](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2019-aeroacoustic-resonance-review.md), p. 134).
- **SPOD → reconstruction → HPOD.** Reconstruct each tone band separately for each symmetry (even and odd fields, or D2 components), then track instantaneous amplitude and frequency. First targets: whether the loops of the multimodal AR 2 jets coexist or alternate ([Mazharmanesh 2025](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2025-screech-modes-non-axisymmetric-jets.md); [Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md)), diagonal against minor-axis flapping ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md)), and helix against alternating flapping and wagging in mode V of the thick-lipped ellipse ([Edgington-Mitchell 2015, staging](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2015-staging-screeching-elliptical-jets.md)). Test for beats before calling anything switching ([twin-jet-coupling](twin-jet-coupling.md)). Sub-domain reconstructions either side of the axis switch are on [axis-switching-and-mean-flow](axis-switching-and-mean-flow.md).
- **PyFR staging LES.** For 3-D output the D2 split (or an azimuthal split) cuts memory 4×. If runs reach the |m| = 1 stages, round-jet LES can carry slowly rotating |m| = 2 streaks that fix a flapping axis, and averaging them out needs records several times longer than one streak rotation ([Nogueira 2026](../Notes/nogueira-2026-flapping-motion-screeching-round-jets.md), p. 14).
