# Shock-cell models and shock–wavepacket interaction

_Last updated: 2026-10-23 · 19 papers_

## Scope

Models of the mean shock-cell train of an imperfectly expanded jet and of its interaction with wavepackets: the Prandtl–Pack vortex sheet and its extensions (any cross-section, multiple scales, zero-frequency PSE, RANS- and PIV-derived trains), spacing, its downstream shrinkage and its damping by screech, the multi-peak shock spectrum (k_s1, k_s2, k_s3), the Tam & Tanna product picture (k ± k_s), Floquet stability periodic in x, shock modulation of wavepackets (PFE), and the shock side of broadband shock-associated noise (BBSAN) sources, wavepacket and statistical.

Linked, not repeated: [screech](screech.md); [screech frequency and global modes](screech-frequency-and-global-modes.md); [screech staging and mode switching](screech-staging-and-mode-switching.md); BBSAN spectra, source locations and azimuthal content in [jet-noise mechanisms](jet-noise-mechanisms.md); global and harmonic-resolvent analyses in [linear operator methods](linear-operator-methods.md); vortex sheets and guided-jet waves in [linear models](linear-models.md); [non-axisymmetric jets](non-axisymmetric-jets.md); full-scale shock noise in [rocket-noise measurements](rocket-noise-measurements.md). Spacings are tabulated in `Data/shock_cell_spacing.csv` ([Data/README](../Data/README.md)).

## Current understanding

1. **A vortex sheet gets the first cells right.** Pack's solution gives L_s ≈ 1.31(M_j² − 1)^{1/2} D_j ([Tam & Tanna 1982](../Notes/tam-1982-shock-associated-noise-cd-nozzles.md), p. 6–7); Tam's version for any cross-section matches choked rectangular jets ([Tam 1988](../Notes/tam-1988-shock-cell-screech-rectangular-non-axisymmetric-jets.md), p. 9–10) and an AR 2 LES first cell within 1–5% ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 15).
2. **Downstream the cells shrink and decay**, about 6% per cell ([Mancinelli 2019](../Notes/mancinelli-2019-screech-prediction-upstream-modes.md), p. 4), to two-thirds of the first by the seventh in PIV at M_j 1.45 ([Tan 2019a](../Notes/tan-2019-correlation-analysis-piv-screeching-jets.md), p. 20, note's reading). Multiple-scales and zero-frequency PSE models capture this for five cells or more ([Ray 2005](../Notes/ray-2005-instability-wave-shock-cell-interaction-sound.md), p. 6–7; [Nogueira 2022, PFE](../Notes/nogueira-2021-wavepacket-modulation-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 8–9, 16–17); the vortex sheet cannot (Tam 1988, p. 10).
3. **Screech itself damps the mean train.** A notched-lip, screech-free twin keeps the first cells, but its train lasts about twice as far ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), p. 667–668). Linear trains thus describe screech-free jets; screeching ones need a mean-flow correction, such as the harmonic resolvent's St = 0 response ([Farghadan 2026](../Notes/farghadan-2026-wave-interactions-screeching-jet.md), p. 20–21).
4. **So the shock spectrum has several peaks**: k_s1 from the near-uniform cells and suboptimal k_s2, k_s3 from the shrinking ones, which are not Pack's higher modes ([Nogueira 2022, A1/A2](../Notes/nogueira-2021-closure-mechanism-a1-a2-screech.md), p. 7–8; [Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 7–8, 17). The "second mode" of BBSAN models, at 2–2.3k_s1, is a different wavenumber.
5. **KH × shock gives waves at k ± k_s.** The difference wave can be supersonic and radiate as Mach waves ([Tam & Tanna 1982](../Notes/tam-1982-shock-associated-noise-cd-nozzles.md), p. 8–9); the products were confirmed in PIV with a linear operator ([Edgington-Mitchell 2021](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2020-waves-in-screeching-jets.md), p. 15–19, 24), and k_kh − k_s feeds the guided jet mode that closes screech ([Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 12–13).
6. **Periodic shocks create an absolute instability** within 2–6% of the screech frequency ([Nogueira 2021](../Notes/nogueira-2021-absolute-instability-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 13–15), necessary but not sufficient for a global mode ([Huerre & Monkewitz 1990](../Notes/huerre-1990-local-global-instabilities-spatially-developing-flows.md), p. 492–495).
7. **BBSAN needs the wavepacket envelope and coherence, not only k_s** ([Ray 2005](../Notes/ray-2005-instability-wave-shock-cell-interaction-sound.md), p. 5–7; [Wong 2021](../Notes/wong-2021-wavepacket-modelling-bbsan.md), p. 20–25). Coherence decay lowers the peak by 2–5 dB rather than amplifying it ([Wong 2019](../Notes/wong-2019-coherence-decay-wavepacket-bbsan.md), p. 978), and shock harmonics lengthen the envelope ([Nogueira 2022, PFE](../Notes/nogueira-2021-wavepacket-modulation-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 18–19).
8. **Spacing formulas fit moderately underexpanded jets only.** Pack is 7–11% too long behind a Mach disc at M_j 1.59 ([Tan 2019a](../Notes/tan-2019-correlation-analysis-piv-screeching-jets.md), p. 20–21; [Tan 2019b](../Notes/tan-2019-equivalent-bbsan-source-reconstruction-screeching-jets.md), p. 35) and probably at low β ([Wong 2020](../Notes/wong-2020-azimuthal-decomposition-radiated-noise-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 2019–2020); for the overexpanded F404 no formula fits, and the BBSAN peak formula works only with measured spacing ([Mathews 2025](../Notes/mathews-2025-thesis-supersonic-jet-rocket-noise-saturn.md), p. 34, 36, 40).

## Vortex-sheet, multiple-scales and PSE shock cells

**Prandtl–Pack.** Prandtl's vortex-sheet cell length equals the acoustic modes of a gas column (Rayleigh) and is shorter than the diamond spacing of choked jets ([Raman 1998](../Notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 58). In Pack's version, as used by [Tam & Tanna 1982](../Notes/tam-1982-shock-associated-noise-cd-nozzles.md), the shock pressure is a sum of J₀(2μ_n r/D_j) cos(λ_n x) modes with amplitudes set by the exit pressure mismatch, with no viscosity, spreading, decay or Mach discs; the first mode sets the spacing (p. 6–7), and the λ₂ wave is 7.2 dB below it (p. 9). [Tam 1995](../Notes/tam-1995-supersonic-jet-noise.md) reads the cells as waveguide modes trapped by the mixing layer (p. 30–32), and [Mancinelli 2021](../Notes/mancinelli-2021-complex-valued-resonance-model-screech.md) recovers Prandtl–Pack as the ω → 0 limit of the vortex-sheet dispersion relation (p. 7–8), the same family as the guided and duct modes ([linear models](linear-models.md)).

**Any cross-section.** [Tam 1988](../Notes/tam-1988-shock-cell-screech-rectangular-non-axisymmetric-jets.md) turns the problem into a 2-D Helmholtz eigenproblem over the fully expanded cross-section, whose smallest eigenvalue fixes L_s (p. 4–5). For rectangles the minor dimension sets the spacing, with a correction under 3% above AR 4 (p. 6). The formula matches choked AR 5.8 and 12.8 jets over M_j 1.1–1.7, beyond the expected linear range, because the measured cells are the first ones, where the layer is thin (p. 9–10). AR < 4 could not be tested (p. 12); an AR 2 LES later found first cells at 0.95–0.99 of Tam's length for temperature ratios 1–3 ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 15). Rectangular comparisons are on [non-axisymmetric jets](non-axisymmetric-jets.md).

**Shrinkage and decay.** Four fixes for what the vortex sheet misses:
- *Empirical.* About 6% shorter per cell (α = 0.06; [Mancinelli 2019](../Notes/mancinelli-2019-screech-prediction-upstream-modes.md), p. 4; [Mancinelli 2021](../Notes/mancinelli-2021-complex-valued-resonance-model-screech.md), p. 7–8), 5.5% in the AR 2 LES ([Gojon 2019](../Notes/gojon-2019-antisymmetric-oscillation-modes-rectangular-screeching-jets.md), p. 15), or a 0.8 factor for cells near the end of the core ([Tam 1986](../Notes/tam-1986-bbsan-screech-relationship.md), p. 11; [Tam 1995](../Notes/tam-1995-supersonic-jet-noise.md), p. 37). The α rule places the 4th shock within 10% of PIV (Mancinelli 2021, p. 22). PIV reflection points of the group's C-mode jets are near-equal for three cells, then close in, from 1.51 to 0.99D apart at M_j 1.45 and from 1.70 to 1.34D at M_j 1.59 ([Tan 2019a](../Notes/tan-2019-correlation-analysis-piv-screeching-jets.md), note's reading of Fig. 2, p. 20–21).
- *Multiple scales.* Finite thickness shortens the spacing by about 20% over the core ([Raman 1998](../Notes/raman-1998-supersonic-jet-screech-review.md), p. 59). Tam, Jackson & Seiner's linear multiple-scales model matches Norum & Seiner's axial pressures in spacing and amplitude ([Tam 1995](../Notes/tam-1995-supersonic-jet-noise.md), p. 32; Raman 1998, p. 59–61). On a RANS mean its first three zero-frequency modes reproduce the shock train of a mildly overexpanded jet; the higher modes carry the near-nozzle fine structure, and near the end of the core only the first matters ([Ray 2005](../Notes/ray-2005-instability-wave-shock-cell-interaction-sound.md), p. 6–7).
- *Zero-frequency PSE.* The ω = m = 0 march on a modelled mean flow gives duct-like modes matching Pack; shear-layer growth raises α_s and decays the shocks. Spacing and amplitude after the first cell match LES to x/D ≈ 6, but the first-cell Mach reflection is missed ([Nogueira 2022, PFE](../Notes/nogueira-2021-wavepacket-modulation-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 8–9, 16–17).
- *Measured or computed train.* [Wong 2021](../Notes/wong-2021-wavepacket-modelling-bbsan.md) fits Pack's wavenumbers and axial amplitudes to the PIV shock perturbation, taken as the shock-containing PIV mean minus the ideally expanded LES mean (p. 9–11), and then matches it everywhere but in the shear layer (p. 15). [Tan 2019b](../Notes/tan-2019-equivalent-bbsan-source-reconstruction-screeching-jets.md) rebuilds the shock pressure from the mean PIV velocity (eqs. 13–20, p. 17–18); a k–ω SST RANS matches it beyond x/D ≈ 1.5 but drifts beyond x/D ≈ 5 at M_j 1.59, where it neglects the Mach disc's excitation of the shocks (p. 30–33).

**Screech shortens the train.** PNS-type solvers all miss the rapid decay in strongly screeching jets, whereas for moderate mismatch linear cells with an empirical initial amplitude suffice for BBSAN ([Tam 1995](../Notes/tam-1995-supersonic-jet-noise.md), p. 32); Norum & Seiner's benchmark trains decay by x/d ≈ 7–9 ([Norum & Seiner 1982](../Notes/norum-1982-mean-static-pressure-far-field-acoustics-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 14, 27). Screech is the cause ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md)): shallow lip notches suppress it but leave the first cells, their spacing (≈ 1.2D at M_j 1.35, note's reading of Fig. 5, as [Mercier 2017](../Notes/mercier-2017-screech-feedback-loop-underexpanded-round-jets.md) found on the same rig, p. 222) and their strength almost unchanged, and the centreline oscillation then lasts to x/D ≈ 11 instead of collapsing just downstream of 5.3, where strong screech sets in (p. 667–668). Linear, multiple-scales and PFE trains should be compared with screech-free data or corrected (note's reading), for example by the harmonic resolvent's St = 0 response, a shock-cell change confined to where the screech mode is active ([Farghadan 2026](../Notes/farghadan-2026-wave-interactions-screeching-jet.md), p. 20–21).

**Where the formulas fail.** In an afterburning, overexpanded F404 (M_j 1.46, M_d 1.55) the spacing falls from about 0.97 to 0.72 D_j over cells II–VIII (note's reading of Fig. 5) and matches neither Prandtl–Pack, Harper-Bourne & Fisher's decaying spacing nor a β-generalised Prandtl–Pack, which fit underexpanded jets ([Mathews 2025](../Notes/mathews-2025-thesis-supersonic-jet-rocket-noise-saturn.md), p. 34). No formula captures the larger spacing of underexpanded jets (p. 35); temperature ratio matters less than over- or underexpansion and β (p. 34). Behind a Mach disc Pack is too long: at M_j 1.59 the first cells are 7–11% shorter, depending on which are averaged, while at M_j 1.45 the first interval matches Pack's 1.47D and the next four average 1.37D ([Tan 2019a](../Notes/tan-2019-correlation-analysis-piv-screeching-jets.md), p. 20–21; [Tan 2019b](../Notes/tan-2019-equivalent-bbsan-source-reconstruction-screeching-jets.md), p. 35; notes' readings), as the same data's k_s gives ([Edgington-Mitchell 2021](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2020-waves-in-screeching-jets.md), p. 16–17). With Pack's L₁ and u_c = 0.7U_j the peak formula fits the BBSAN lobes at M_j 1.38 and 1.50 but under-predicts the peak at M_j 1.14 (β 0.55), which the authors blame on the spacing ([Wong 2020](../Notes/wong-2020-azimuthal-decomposition-radiated-noise-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 2019–2020); no spacing was measured.

## The shock spectrum: one wavenumber or several

Tam & Tanna expected weak higher components in a uniform train, though finer structure within each cell strengthens the second in coannular jets ([Tam & Tanna 1982](../Notes/tam-1982-shock-associated-noise-cd-nozzles.md), p. 9–10); BBSAN peaks sit at the shock Fourier wavenumbers k_n ([Tam 1986](../Notes/tam-1986-bbsan-screech-relationship.md), p. 4). In [Nogueira 2022, A1/A2](../Notes/nogueira-2021-closure-mechanism-a1-a2-screech.md) one k_sh fits the centreline mean pressure only for x/D < 4, while a chirp with k_sh rising downstream fits the whole domain and reproduces secondary peaks (p. 7–8). Both peaks fall with M_j as Pack predicts, their difference stays roughly constant, and the secondary peaks are not Pack's higher harmonics (p. 8). [Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md) found the same in mean schlieren from two facilities, plus harmonics of k_s1 (p. 7–8). A two-cell sliding window recovers k_s1 from the first cells and k_s2 from windows starting at the 5th to 8th cell, so closure is really k_G = k_KH − k_sL for a local quasi-periodicity k_sL (p. 17). At AR 4, flapping breaks the shocks down by the third cell, leaving one broad peak and no staging (p. 18–19).

Two different "second" wavenumbers are in use: the staging k_s2 lies close to k_s1 (k_s = 10.7, 12.3, 13.2 in [Farghadan 2026](../Notes/farghadan-2026-wave-interactions-screeching-jet.md), p. 15–16), whereas the second BBSAN lobe needs one near 2k_s1. [Wong 2019](../Notes/wong-2019-coherence-decay-wavepacket-bbsan.md) uses Pack's uniform modes (k_s2 = 2.30k_s1) with amplitudes lacking Tam & Tanna's radial factor, so n = 2 enters at +0.44 of n = 1 rather than −0.66 on the axis (eqs. 2.5–2.7, p. 974; note's check), and needs ten modes to converge (p. 984); [Wong 2021](../Notes/wong-2021-wavepacket-modelling-bbsan.md) shifts them with a PIV fit (eq. 4.6, p. 11). The curves of [Wong 2020](../Notes/wong-2020-azimuthal-decomposition-radiated-noise-shock-containing-jets.md) are harmonics instead, L_n = L₁/n (note's check of Fig. 3), which the measured lobes match "favourably" (p. 2020), untested against 2.30.

The nozzle alone does not set the peaks. k_s2 is weak in the flapping B mode and strong otherwise ([Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 8–9, 11–12), and the spectrum jumps where the stage changes, which the authors read as two-way coupling (p. 12). [Mazharmanesh 2026](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2026-triadic-interaction-mean-flow-deformation-screech.md) agrees that k_s is tied to where the triad happens, against models that take it from the limit-cycle mean (p. 19), yet finds k_s1 and k_s2 within 1% between post-switch and long-time means (p. 20). The zero-frequency harmonic-resolvent response, with peaks at k_s2, k_s3 and their combinations, is read the same way by [Farghadan 2026](../Notes/farghadan-2026-wave-interactions-screeching-jet.md) (p. 22–23). In the far field a screeching jet's BBSAN peak follows 2St_s through every stage jump, and the convection speed from BBSAN directivity varies with M_j only with screech ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), p. 669–670). The authors suspect stage-dependent spacing or convection speed; the note suggests harmonic radiation, since f_p(90°) = 2f_s is where k_kh(2St_s) ≈ k_s1 (Farghadan 2026, p. 23).

## The product picture

[Tam & Tanna 1982](../Notes/tam-1982-shock-associated-noise-cd-nozzles.md) multiplied an instability wave of wavenumber k by Pack's cos(λ_n x) series, giving waves at k ± λ_n (p. 5, 8–9). Only k − λ₁ radiates well: k + λ_n is subsonic and k − λ₂ weak (p. 9). The Mach-angle condition gives Harper-Bourne & Fisher's peak frequency, f_p = u_c/[L_s(1 − M_c cos θ)] (p. 9), within one third-octave band at 75–135° with u_c = 0.7u_j (p. 16–18), though the authors called the model "still quite tentative" (p. 21). [Tam 1995](../Notes/tam-1995-supersonic-jet-noise.md) gives one BBSAN peak per shock-cell mode and rules out plane shock–turbulence interaction (p. 30, 32–34). At θ = 180° the product reaches the lip: the weakest-link screech frequency ([Tam 1988](../Notes/tam-1988-shock-cell-screech-rectangular-non-axisymmetric-jets.md), p. 10). With jittering wavepackets the sum and mixed terms stay silent ([Wong 2019](../Notes/wong-2019-coherence-decay-wavepacket-bbsan.md), p. 983). The shocks in these models are axisymmetric and steady, so the wavepacket carries all azimuthal content: one convection speed fits every azimuthal mode, and m up to about 4 is needed at St ≳ 0.7 ([Wong 2020](../Notes/wong-2020-azimuthal-decomposition-radiated-noise-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 2020, 2022–2023).

It was confirmed experimentally with a linear operator: once the shocks are part of the mean flow, the screech loop is linear except for the KH–shock interaction ([Edgington-Mitchell 2021](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2020-waves-in-screeching-jets.md), p. 15–19, 24). The subsonic sum waves matter inside the jet, as core-confined duct-mode peaks at k_kh + k_s1 ([Farghadan 2026](../Notes/farghadan-2026-wave-interactions-screeching-jet.md), p. 22–23). The k_kh − k_s product feeds the upstream guided jet mode on k_s1 or k_s2 depending on the stage ([Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 12–13), while the upstream sound comes from energy leaked to supersonic wavenumbers, as in Tam & Tanna (p. 13–14). Global and harmonic-resolvent versions are on [linear operator methods](linear-operator-methods.md).

## Floquet models periodic in x

[Nogueira 2021](../Notes/nogueira-2021-absolute-instability-shock-containing-jets.md) modulates a tanh profile with the leading term of Pack's series at Pack's spacing (p. 7). A 2% sinusoidal shock barely moves the eigenvalues (< 0.01%, p. 8) but creates a KH–guided-mode saddle that meets the Briggs–Bers pinch condition (p. 14). The saddle frequency is insensitive to shock amplitude, while its growth rate ω₀,ᵢ depends strongly on shear-layer thickness δ (p. 15–16). The growth rates are not quantitative: the KH growth had to be cut to 20% to compare mode shapes (p. 11–12). [Nogueira 2022, A1/A2](../Notes/nogueira-2021-closure-mechanism-a1-a2-screech.md) feeds in the two measured shock wavenumbers. Its saddles fall within 10% of A1 and A2 (p. 12), and ω₀,ᵢ ranks A1 above A2 at M_j = 1.12 (0.223 vs 0.057; p. 13). The model over-predicts A2 at M_j = 1.22, where the measured tone is weak (p. 14). How a local pocket relates to a global mode is on [linear operator methods](linear-operator-methods.md).

## Shock modulation of the wavepacket

The parabolised Floquet equations (PFE) of [Nogueira 2022, PFE](../Notes/nogueira-2021-wavepacket-modulation-shock-containing-jets.md) add zero-frequency PSE shocks to a modelled ideally expanded mean and march a KH wavenumber α with harmonics α ± jα_s, starting from the spatially periodic problem without ∂/∂x (p. 6). The harmonics are slaved to KH, the only structure that draws energy from the mean flow (p. 12). In shock-containing jets the envelope is longer than that of shock-free PSE, which decay too fast (p. 18–19). PFE and SPOD spectra share several peaks and carry energy at negative, acoustically matched wavenumbers, consistent with BBSAN from wavepacket–shock modulation (p. 20). The stated limits are a modelled mean flow, a linear duct-like shock train, truncated harmonics and no forcing or eddy viscosity (p. 20–21); marching limits are on [linear operator methods](linear-operator-methods.md).

Screech-filtered PIV shows the shocks modulating the turbulence too: at the reflection points coherent and random energy are in anti-phase and the integral scale L₁₁ peaks ([Tan 2019a](../Notes/tan-2019-correlation-analysis-piv-screeching-jets.md), p. 22, 32–33), as the coherent and random stresses peak just upstream and downstream of them in the same rig ([Edgington-Mitchell 2014](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2014-coherent-structure-helical-screech-mode.md), p. 836–837). These are turbulence statistics, not the KH envelope.

## Shock-cell sources of BBSAN

[Ray 2005](../Notes/ray-2005-instability-wave-shock-cell-interaction-sound.md) first drove a linearised Euler solver with products of a PSE wave and zero-frequency shock modes on a RANS mean (p. 2–3). At St 0.6 the upstream lobe sits at the local Mach angle of k_t − k_s, and a locally parallel Green's function matches the solver in level and direction (p. 9); at St 0.9 ring sources split the radiation into narrow lobes whose angles, but not amplitudes, it reproduces (p. 9–10, 12–13). Level and directivity thus depend on the wavepacket's radial shape and envelope length, not only on its wavenumber (p. 5–7). The forced solver also radiates Mach waves from KH waves it excites itself (p. 7), and the study has no comparison with measurements (p. 13).

[Wong 2019](../Notes/wong-2019-coherence-decay-wavepacket-bbsan.md) added jitter to a kinematic line source of Gaussian wavepacket × Pack modes (p. 973–976). Coherence decay lowers the peak by 2–5 dB and broadens the directivity, because the matched k_h − k_s lobe already lies inside the radiation square (p. 978, 980–983); with ten shock modes it fills the upstream high-frequency "missing sound" of Ray & Lele (2007) (p. 980, 984–988). Neither the envelope length nor the coherence length moves the peak frequency, but a longer envelope sharpens the spurious secondary peaks (p. 988–990), so shock-lengthened envelopes would need more jitter (our inference).

The sequel, [Wong 2021](../Notes/wong-2021-wavepacket-modelling-bbsan.md), builds the source from flow data alone. Fully coherent PSE sources put the n = 1 and n = 2 peaks within ±2 dB/St, and adding LES coherence decay (jitter) recovers up to 15 dB/St between the peaks (p. 20–25). The source lies inside the plume, strongest between the 3rd and 5th cells (p. 27). At full scale the F404's shock-noise sources sit at cells II–VIII, with cell I silent and VI–VII strongest ([Mathews 2025](../Notes/mathews-2025-thesis-supersonic-jet-rocket-noise-saturn.md), p. 32), and their coherence suggests one wavepacket interacting with several shocks (p. 39).

The statistical route of [Tan 2019b](../Notes/tan-2019-equivalent-bbsan-source-reconstruction-screeching-jets.md) has no wavepacket: Morris & Miller's analogy multiplies the measured shock pressure by a separable correlation of screech-filtered PIV or RANS turbulence (p. 9–11). Its shock time scale, τ̂₂ ∝ |∂V̄₁/∂x₁|⁻¹ ([Tan 2019a](../Notes/tan-2019-correlation-analysis-piv-screeching-jets.md), p. 10–11), is inactive except near the first reflection (p. 33; Tan 2019b, p. 20–21), so the cells set where the source sits, not its time scale: on the sonic line just upstream of each reflection, mostly in cells 1–5 (Tan 2019b, p. 36–38), a location the local product partly builds in (note's reading). Without screech the downstream cells are the strongest, highest-frequency sources ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), p. 669–671), so trains measured in screeching jets, such as Wong 2021's (p. 9–11), under-represent them (note's reading). Source locations are compared on [jet-noise mechanisms](jet-noise-mechanisms.md).

## Disagreements and open questions

**Which shock wavenumber closes which stage?** Four answers are on the table:
- A1 uses k_s1 and A2 uses a secondary k_s2 ([Nogueira 2022, A1/A2](../Notes/nogueira-2021-closure-mechanism-a1-a2-screech.md), p. 7–14; [Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md)). In an AR 2 rectangular jet the dominant flapping tone closes on k_sh1, and a k_sh2 loop is offered for the second flapping tone (p. 4218–4220), though its St also lies near the varicose tone ([Nogueira 2025](../Notes/nogueira-2025-screech-military-style-rectangular-nozzles.md), note's reading of Fig. 17).
- The stages differ only by the integer p at one reflection point; App. B of [Mancinelli 2021](../Notes/mancinelli-2021-complex-valued-resonance-model-screech.md) links this to the k_s view (p. 24).
- [Farghadan 2026](../Notes/farghadan-2026-wave-interactions-screeching-jet.md) argues the first suboptimal peak is hidden inside the primary peak (p. 16–17).
- A BMD-derived k_S matches k_kh − k_GJM better than either peak ([Mazharmanesh 2026](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2026-triadic-interaction-mean-flow-deformation-screech.md), p. 20–22).

*To settle:* resolve the shock spectrum over a long field of view and run BMD on time-resolved schlieren across a staging sweep (see [screech staging and mode switching](screech-staging-and-mode-switching.md)). A cheaper check (our inference): map the local Mach angle of k_t − k_s along x, as [Ray 2005](../Notes/ray-2005-instability-wave-shock-cell-interaction-sound.md) did (p. 9), from SPOD k_t(x) and mean k_s(x); 180° is Tam's closure condition.

**Which wavenumber sets the second BBSAN peak?** Pack's second mode at 2.30k_s1 ([Wong 2019](../Notes/wong-2019-coherence-decay-wavepacket-bbsan.md), p. 974; [Wong 2021](../Notes/wong-2021-wavepacket-modelling-bbsan.md), p. 23), or the harmonic 2k_s1 that fits the measured lobes ([Wong 2020](../Notes/wong-2020-azimuthal-decomposition-radiated-noise-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 2020) and appears in mean schlieren ([Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 7–8)? No paper discriminates (see *The shock spectrum*). *To settle (our inference):* the mean shock spectrum and the 90° Strouhal number of the second lobe in one jet; mean schlieren of the SJAF nozzle would also test the M_j 1.14 mismatch (Wong 2020 note).

**Do shocks modulate the KH envelope directly?** PFE says yes: the harmonics are slaved to KH and lengthen the envelope ([Nogueira 2022, PFE](../Notes/nogueira-2021-wavepacket-modulation-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 12, 18–19). [Edgington-Mitchell 2021](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2020-waves-in-screeching-jets.md) (p. 20–23) and [Nogueira 2021](../Notes/nogueira-2021-absolute-instability-shock-containing-jets.md) (p. 16) see superposed separate waves instead. The PFE authors concede they cannot yet separate negative-wavenumber modulation from upstream waves (p. 21). It matters for BBSAN, whose level and directivity depend strongly on envelope length (Ray 2005, p. 5; [Wong 2019](../Notes/wong-2019-coherence-decay-wavepacket-bbsan.md), p. 989). *To settle:* a Floquet OWNS model that carries upstream waves ([linear operator methods](linear-operator-methods.md)), compared with k_x-filtered SPOD at frequencies away from screech.

**One-way or two-way coupling?** Models take k_s from the limit-cycle mean, yet k_s2 strength and the far-field BBSAN peak change with the stage, the harmonic resolvent reshapes the cells and screech damps the downstream train ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), p. 667–669), while conditional means barely move k_s1 and k_s2 (see *The shock spectrum*). *To settle (our inference):* conditional mean shock spectra per stage from long intermittent records, or from converged A1 and A2 LES states at one NPR; bicoherence or BMD at (St_s, St_s) would test whether harmonic radiation, not a changed train, moves the BBSAN peak (André note).

**Which shock model where Pack fails?** No spacing formula works for the overexpanded F404 ([Mathews 2025](../Notes/mathews-2025-thesis-supersonic-jet-rocket-noise-saturn.md), p. 34–35) or behind a Mach disc (see *Where the formulas fail*). Multiple-scales modes on a RANS mean reproduced a mildly overexpanded jet (β ≈ 0.78) for about five cells ([Ray 2005](../Notes/ray-2005-instability-wave-shock-cell-interaction-sound.md), p. 6–7), and k–ω SST RANS holds a Mach-disc jet's spacing to x/D ≈ 5 ([Tan 2019b](../Notes/tan-2019-equivalent-bbsan-source-reconstruction-screeching-jets.md), p. 30); both are candidates (our reading). *To settle (our inference):* zero-frequency PSE or multiple scales on screech-free measured means of one C-D nozzle across over- and underexpansion.

**Jitter or shock unsteadiness between the BBSAN peaks?** Tam (1987) blamed shock unsteadiness for the dips; [Wong 2021](../Notes/wong-2021-wavepacket-modelling-bbsan.md) finds most broadening is wavepacket jitter, leaving only residual dips to unsteady shocks (p. 25), and [Wong 2019](../Notes/wong-2019-coherence-decay-wavepacket-bbsan.md) had filled the gaps by coherence decay alone, with steady shocks (p. 974, 984–987). Yet the shocks of screeching jets oscillate at the screech frequency ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), p. 672), which these models omit. *To settle (our inference):* measure shock oscillation in time-resolved schlieren, and compare the gaps of screeching and screech-free twins.

## Methods and data

- **Shock train.** Mean fields suffice, so non-time-resolved data are enough: mean schlieren, BOS, PIV, probe pressure, or static-pressure surveys ([Norum & Seiner 1982](../Notes/norum-1982-mean-static-pressure-far-field-acoustics-shock-containing-jets.md), App. A). The centreline FFT needs a long field of view and a sliding window to place each peak ([Edgington-Mitchell 2022](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2022-unifying-theory-jet-screech.md), p. 7, 17; [Nogueira 2022, A1/A2](../Notes/nogueira-2021-closure-mechanism-a1-a2-screech.md), p. 7–8). The shock perturbation can be isolated as measured minus ideally expanded mean ([Wong 2021](../Notes/wong-2021-wavepacket-modelling-bbsan.md), p. 9–11) or the shock pressure rebuilt from mean PIV velocity ([Tan 2019b](../Notes/tan-2019-equivalent-bbsan-source-reconstruction-screeching-jets.md), p. 17–18).
- **Screech-free reference.** A notched-lip twin that keeps the first cells; tabs also shorten the spacing ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), p. 665–668).
- **Spacing table.** `Data/shock_cell_spacing.csv`: 124 rows (89 experiment, 5 LES, 30 model) for round, rectangular, elliptic and twin jets, each with its L_s definition.
- **Interaction.** Time-resolved schlieren or LES: k_x spectra of SPOD modes show the k_kh ± k_s products, and BMD interaction maps give the active shock wavenumber ([Mazharmanesh 2026](../Notes/mazharmanesh-2026-triadic-interaction-mean-flow-deformation-screech.md), p. 20–22). Snapshot PIV: POD-pair modes and their k_x spectra ([Edgington-Mitchell 2021](../Notes/edgington-mitchell-2020-waves-in-screeching-jets.md)).
- **Cost.** Pack and Tam 1988 are closed-form; SP-LSA takes about 3 min on a laptop ([Nogueira 2021](../Notes/nogueira-2021-absolute-instability-shock-containing-jets.md), p. 6); the PFE minimum step grows with M_j (Nogueira 2022, PFE, p. 6–7).

## Relevance to our work

- **Lab schlieren and acoustics.** Measure k_s1, k_s2 and k_s3 from mean schlieren; compare the first cell with Prandtl–Pack or Tam 1988 and the spacing table, and the shrinkage with 5.5–6% per cell, checking line-of-sight peak positions against PIV where possible (our inference); screeching lab jets decay faster than linear trains ([André 2013](../Notes/andre-2013-bbsan-screeching-non-screeching-underexpanded-jets.md), p. 667–668). For BBSAN peaks in microphone spectra, use measured spacing (Mathews 2025, p. 36, 40), and resolve the mean spectrum near 2k_s1.
- **SPOD → reconstruction → HPOD.** The models assume a steady shock train. One testable link to HPOD amplitudes near a staging boundary is the ω₀,ᵢ ranking of A1 over A2 ([Nogueira 2022, A1/A2](../Notes/nogueira-2021-closure-mechanism-a1-a2-screech.md), p. 13); conditional mean shock spectra in HPOD-identified A1 and A2 intervals would test two-way coupling (our inference), and short-time mean trains conditioned on HPOD screech amplitude whether the damping scales with screech level (André note).
- **PyFR staging LES.** The mean gives shock positions and k_s(x); the 4th shock checks the α = 0.06 rule, which PIV matched within 10% (Mancinelli 2021, p. 22). At M_j ≈ 1.09–1.14 the centreline-pressure decay can be checked against André's M_j 1.10 case (André note). Converged A1 and A2 states answer the coupling question, and SPOD k_t(x) gives the local Mach-angle map (our inference).
- **Rocket noise.** Heating hardly changes the first shock mode, so it acts through the instability waves, whose gains it raises ([Ray 2005](../Notes/ray-2005-instability-wave-shock-cell-interaction-sound.md), p. 10); Tam & Tanna's cold-to-hot spread is within 3 dB (p. 19–20). Full-scale overexpanded C-D plumes need measured or computed spacing (Mathews 2025, p. 34–35), and screech-free lab trains are the better calibration for non-screeching full-scale jets (André note). See [rocket-noise models](rocket-noise-models.md).
