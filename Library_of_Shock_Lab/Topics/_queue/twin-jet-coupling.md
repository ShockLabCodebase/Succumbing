## bell-2018-coupled-underexpanded-supersonic-twin-jets (queued 2026-10-22)

Bell 2018 is the first paper from the group's PIV enclosure, "facility two" in [Bell 2021](../Notes/bell-2021-intermittent-modal-coupling-screeching-twin-jets.md). It uses the same 10 mm nozzles at s/D 3 as [Knast 2018](../Notes/knast-2018-coupling-modes-underexpanded-twin-axisymmetric-jet.md). Its NPR 4.6 and 5.0 PIV sets (about 9000 independent fields each at 0.5 Hz, p. 3) appear to be the data later reanalysed by [Nogueira 2021](../Notes/nogueira-2021-twin-jet-coupling-spatial-lsa.md) (NPR 5.0, 1SS) and Bell 2021 (conditional POD). The paper does not say so; the match is the note's inference from rig, NPRs and field counts.
- **Coupling states.** At NPR 5.0, single-reference R11 and R22 maps show steady symmetric coupling: R11 mirror-symmetric, R22 bands opposed across the midplane (Figs 20, 22, p. 12). This agrees with Nogueira's 1SS. At NPR 4.6 there is only a faint symmetric pattern, and no standing wave survives the averages. Of the four explanations offered (p. 15), temporal switching is the one Bell 2021 later confirmed, with three intermittent states.
- **Rig dependence.** In this enclosure the jump to the steady symmetric stage falls between NPR 4.7 and 4.8 (St 0.21 → 0.19, Fig. 3 read; the text says 4.85, p. 3). In the schlieren rig the symmetric process starts at NPR 4.4 (Bell 2021, Fig. 5). This is one more datum for the page's point that identical nozzles stage at different NPRs in different enclosures.
- **New flow-field results:**
  - no inward distortion of the shock cells at s/D 3, unlike s/D 1.9 (Seiner 1986; Goparaju & Gaitonde 2017) (p. 5);
  - first hydrodynamic contact at x/D ≈ 4–4.5 from rms and skewness, against 7.5–8.5 from extrapolating the shear-layer edges (p. 9–11, 17);
  - interior TKE overtakes exterior TKE only beyond x/D ≈ 10 (p. 11);
  - a stronger standing wave between the jets than outside, attributed to in-phase addition of both jets' upstream waves (p. 14).
- **Screech-based coupling.** Panda's relation closes at NPR 5.0 (λ_s,est 3.7 ± 0.17D against 3.9D, Table 2, p. 15), which the authors read as evidence for screech-based coupling. That anticipates Nogueira 2021's guided-mode closure of 1SS on the same data.
- **For "Methods and data".** This paper is the cautionary example for non-time-resolved diagnostics. Log₁₀ rms standing-wave maps (as in Esfahani 2021's schlieren) and single-reference correlation maps work on PIV, but switching between states blurs both.
- **Correction to check.** The Bell 2021 note summarises this paper as finding "clear antisymmetry with a strong near-field standing wave at the steady condition" (Bell 2021, p. 3–4). This paper calls NPR 5.0 symmetric throughout (p. 12, 15, 17), in the same sense as Bell 2021's symmetric and Nogueira's 1SS. If the naming row cites Bell 2018, map it as "symmetric = mirror-symmetric flow (R11), = 1SS".
